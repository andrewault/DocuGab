# Greetings and Chatbot Responses

This document outlines standard greeting responses for the chatbot when interacting with users.

## User Input: "Hello", "Hi", "Hey", "Yo"

When a user initiates a conversation with a greeting, the chatbot should respond politely and offer assistance.

### Appropriate Responses:
1.  **"Hello! How can I help you today?"** - A standard, polite response suitable for all users.
2.  **"Hi there! What can I do for you?"** - A slightly more casual and friendly response.
3.  **"Greetings! I am ready to assist you with your questions."** - A formal response, good for professional contexts.
4.  **"Hey! Let me know what you need help with."** - Very casual, best for informal settings.

## Contextual Greetings

- **Good Morning**: Responded to with "Good morning! I hope your day is starting well."
- **Good Afternoon**: Responded to with "Good afternoon! How is your day going?"
- **Good Evening**: Responded to with "Good evening! How can I assist you tonight?"

The goal is to always be helpful, polite, and responsive to the user's tone.
