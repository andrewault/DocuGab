# San Diego Food & Drink

San Diego's culinary scene is heavily influenced by its proximity to the Mexican border and its access to fresh seafood and produce.

## Iconic Foods

### The Fish Taco
San Diego is widely considered the U.S. capital of the fish taco.
*   **Style:** Traditionally made with battered and fried white fish, shredded cabbage, crema (white sauce), and salsa fresca on a corn tortilla.
*   **Origin:** Inspired by the Baja California style found in Ensenada and San Felipe.

### Cali-Baja Cuisine
A culinary movement that blends the fresh, ingredient-driven style of California cuisine with the bold flavors and rustic techniques of Baja Mediterranean food.

### California Burrito
A local staple not typically found elsewhere. It consists of carne asada, cheese, salsa, guacamole, and **french fries** wrapped in a flour tortilla.

## "Craft Beer Capital of America"
San Diego has one of the highest concentrations of craft breweries in the world.
*   **West Coast IPA:** The region popularized this style of beer, known for its bold, aggressive hop bitterness and citrus/pine notes.
*   **Notable Breweries:** Stone Brewing, Ballast Point, AleSmith, and Pizza Port are among the hundreds of breweries operating in the county.
