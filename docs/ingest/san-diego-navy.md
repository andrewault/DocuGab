# The US Navy in San Diego

San Diego is home to the largest concentration of naval forces in the world and serves as a principal homeport for the Pacific Fleet. The city's deep ties to the Navy date back to the early 20th century.

## Major Installations

### Naval Base San Diego (32nd Street)
Located on the east side of San Diego Bay, this is the principal homeport of the Pacific Fleet, consisting of over 50 ships and more than 150 tenant commands. It is the primary base for surface combatants, including destroyers, cruisers, and littoral combat ships.

### Naval Base Coronado
A consortium of eight installations, including:
*   **Naval Air Station North Island:** Recognized as the "Birthplace of Naval Aviation," it hosts aircraft carriers (like the USS Carl Vinson and USS Theodore Roosevelt) and numerous aviation squadrons.
*   **Naval Amphibious Base Coronado:** The headquarters for the Naval Special Warfare Command and the training ground for the Navy SEALs.

### Naval Base Point Loma
Situated at the mouth of the bay, this base serves as a home for major submarine squadrons and the Third Fleet headquarters.

## Strategic Importance
San Diego's strategic location on the Pacific Rim makes it a vital hub for projecting U.S. naval power. The region provides ideal deep-water ports and extensive training ranges (sea, air, and land) that are crucial for fleet readiness.

## Economic Impact
The military is a cornerstone of San Diego's economy. It is the top employer in the county, accounting for nearly 25% of all jobs in the region. The defense industry supports thousands of businesses and drives innovation in technology and aerospace.
