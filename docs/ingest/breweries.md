
# Breweries in San Diego

San Diego is widely regarded as one of the craft beer capitals of the world. With over 150 operational breweries, the county has a rich history of brewing excellence, particularly known for defining the West Coast IPA style.

## Key Brewing Hubs

### North Park
Often considered the heart of the San Diego beer scene, North Park is densely packed with taprooms and breweries, making it perfect for a walking brewery tour.
- **Modern Times Beer**: Known for their coffee stouts, hazy IPAs, and eclectic, retro-futuristic decor.
- **North Park Beer Co.**: Highly acclaimed for their hop-forward IPAs and lagers.
- **Fall Brewing Company**: A punk rock-themed brewery known for its "Plenty for All" Pilsner and solid lineup of clear IPAs.

### Miramar ("Beeramar")
An industrial area that houses many of the larger production facilities and their attached tasting rooms.
- **AleSmith Brewing Company**: World-renowned for their Speedway Stout and classic San Diego Pale Ale .394.
- **Ballast Point**: One of the pioneers of the industry, famous for Sculpin IPA.
- **Pure Project**: Focuses on murky IPAs and barrel-aged sours with a strong commitment to sustainability.
- **Societe Brewing Company**: A brewer's brewery, famous for "The Pupil" IPA and "The Harlot" blonde ale.
- **Harland Brewing Co.**: Known for their clean Japanese Lagers and adventurous milkshake IPAs.

### Vista / North County
Home to some of the industry giants and a thriving local scene.
- **Stone Brewing**: The gargantuan brewery that put Arrogant Bastard and aggressive West Coast IPAs on the map.
- **The Lost Abbey**: venerated for their Belgian-inspired ales and barrel-aging program.
- **Pizza Port**: A local legend combining great pizza with GABF medal-winning beers; a true incubator for brewing talent.
- **Burgeon Beer Company**: Nature-inspired brewery in Carlsbad making some of the freshest IPAs in the county.
- **Belching Beaver**: Famous for their Peanut Butter Milk Stout and fun, accessible branding.

### Downtown & The Coast
San Diego’s brewing culture extends to its city center and beautiful coastline.
- **Karl Strauss Brewing Company**: The pioneer that started it all in 1989, sparking San Diego's craft beer revolution.
- **Coronado Brewing Company**: Offering "Mermaid's Red" and "Islander IPA" with a distinct island vibe.
- **Eppig Brewing**: Known for having one of the most scenic waterfront patios in Point Loma and brewing technically perfect lagers.
- **Resident Brewing**: A high-quality brewery tucked inside The Local eatery in downtown, known for "Vacation" Coconut IPA.

## Notable Styles
While you can find any style of beer in San Diego, the region is most famous for the **San Diego-style IPA**—a subset of the West Coast IPA that is typically very pale, super dry, and incredibly hop-forward with high bitterness and citrus/pine aromatics.

Whether you're looking for a crisp lager by the beach or a heavy barrel-aged stout, San Diego's diverse brewery landscape has something to offer every beer enthusiast.
