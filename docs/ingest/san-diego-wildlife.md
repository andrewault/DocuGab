# Wildlife of San Diego

San Diego's diverse ecosystems, ranging from coastal waters and chaparral to mountains and deserts, support a wide variety of wildlife.

## Marine Life

### Seals and Sea Lions
One of the most famous sights in La Jolla are the **Pacific Harbor Seals** at the Children's Pool and the **California Sea Lions** at La Jolla Cove. They can be seen hauling out on the rocks and beaches to rest and warm themselves.

### Gray Whales
From December to April, **Pacific Gray Whales** migrate past the San Diego coast on their way from Alaska to Baja California. They are frequently spotted from coastal cliffs like Torrey Pines and Point Loma.

### Leopard Sharks
During the summer months, hundreds of harmless **Leopard Sharks** congregate in the shallow waters of La Jolla Shores to breed.

## Land Mammals

### Coyotes
**Coyotes** are common throughout San Diego County, including in urban canyons. They are adaptable predators that play a key role in controlling rodent populations.

### Mule Deer
**Southern Mule Deer** are frequently seen in the foothills and mountains, particularly at dawn and dusk.

### Mountain Lions
Although elusive and rarely seen, **Mountain Lions** inhabit the larger open space reserves and mountain ranges of the county.

## Reptiles

### Rattlesnakes
San Diego is home to several species of rattlesnakes, including the **Southern Pacific Rattlesnake**. They are most active in the warmer months and can be found on hiking trails in open space areas.
