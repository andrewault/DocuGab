# Major Events in San Diego

San Diego hosts several massive annual events that draw visitors from around the world.

## Pop Culture

### San Diego Comic-Con International
Held effectively every July at the San Diego Convention Center.
*   **What is it?** The largest comic book and popular culture convention in the world.
*   **Impact:** It takes over downtown San Diego, with Hollywood studios presenting major movie and TV reveals.

## Cultural & Seasonal

### December Nights
Held in Balboa Park during the first weekend of December.
*   **What is it?** A massive free holiday festival featuring light displays, music, and food from around the world at the International Cottages.

### San Diego Pride
Held in July in Hillcrest and Balboa Park.
*   **What is it?** One of the largest Pride parades and festivals in the United States, celebrating the LGBTQ+ community.

## Fair & Air Shows

### San Diego County Fair
Held in June and July at the Del Mar Fairgrounds.
*   **What is it?** A classic county fair with rides, exhibits, and fried food, but on a massive scale (drawing over 1.5 million visitors).

### MCAS Miramar Air Show
Held annually (usually September) at the Marine Corps Air Station Miramar.
*   **What is it?** The largest military air show in the United States, featuring the Blue Angels and demonstrations of military aircraft.
