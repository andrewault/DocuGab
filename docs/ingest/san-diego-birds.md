# Native Birds of San Diego

San Diego is a birdwatcher's paradise, home to over 500 species due to its position on the Pacific Flyway.

## Coastal Birds

### California Brown Pelican
*   **Description:** A large seabird with a long beak and throat pouch.
*   **Behavior:** Famous for their dramatic plunge-dives from high air to catch fish.
*   **Where to find:** Glide along the coastlines of La Jolla and Sunset Cliffs, or resting on pier pilings.

### Double-crested Cormorant
*   **Description:** A matte-black seabird with yellow-orange skin on the face.
*   **Behavior:** Often seen standing on rocks or pilings with its wings spread out to dry.
*   **Where to find:** Common in San Diego Bay, Mission Bay, and along the coast.

### Osprey
*   **Description:** A large raptor, also known as a "sea hawk.
*   **Behavior:** They hunt exclusively for fish and build massive stick nests on top of poles and platforms.
*   **Where to find:** Coastal lagoons like Batiquitos and San Elijo, and around Mission Bay.

## Scrub and Chaparral Birds

### California Gnatcatcher
*   **Description:** A tiny, blue-gray bird.
*   **Behavior:** It is a threatened species that relies specifically on coastal sage scrub habitat. It has a kitten-like "mew" call.
*   **Where to find:** San Dieguito River Park and Torrey Pines State Reserve.

### California Towhee
*   **Description:** A plain brown bird with rust-colored undertail coverts.
*   **Behavior:** Often seen scratching on the ground in leaf litter searching for seeds.
*   **Where to find:** Common in gardens, parks, and chaparral throughout the county.

### Greater Roadrunner
*   **Description:** A large, ground-dwelling cuckoo with a long tail and crest.
*   **Behavior:** Famous for running rapidly across the ground; it hunts lizards, snakes, and insects.
*   **Where to find:** Open chaparral and desert edges; frequently seen in Mission Trails Regional Park.

### Red-tailed Hawk
*   **Description:** Large hawk with broad wings and a signature brick-red tail.
*   **Behavior:** Soars in high circles or perches on telephone poles scanning for prey.
*   **Where to find:** Widespread across all open habitats in the county.

### Turkey Vulture
*   **Description:** Large dark bird with a bald red head.
*   **Behavior:** Soars unsteadily on V-shaped wings, using its keen sense of smell to find carrion.
*   **Where to find:** Soaring over canyons and open spaces throughout the region.

## Urban and Garden Birds

### American Crow
*   **Description:** A large, all-black intelligent bird.
*   **Behavior:** Highly social and often seen in large family groups; known for their problem-solving abilities.
*   **Where to find:** Ubiquitous in urban areas, parks, and neighborhoods.

### Northern Flicker
*   **Description:** A large, brownish woodpecker with a black bib and spotted belly.
*   **Behavior:** Unlike most woodpeckers, it often feeds on the ground, eating ants.
*   **Where to find:** Parks and residential areas with large trees.

### Anna's Hummingbird
*   **Description:** A medium-sized hummingbird; males have a glossy red head and throat.
*   **Behavior:** They are year-round residents and are known for their spectacular dive displays during courtship.
*   **Where to find:** Abundant in backyard gardens and parks with flowering plants.

### Peregrine Falcon
*   **Description:** The fastest animal on the planet.
*   **Behavior:** They are expert hunters of other birds, often catching them in mid-air.
*   **Where to find:** Once endangered, they now nest on high cliffs (like Torrey Pines) and even on the Coronado Bridge.
