# San Diego Neighborhoods

San Diego is defined by its diverse collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own unique vibe and culture.

## Urban & Cultural Hubs

### North Park
Known as the hipster center of San Diego, North Park is famous for its craft breweries, coffee shops, and Craftsman cottages. The Ray Street Arts District and the historic North Park Theatre are local landmarks.

### Little Italy
Originally a fishing village, this hilly neighborhood is now a bustling culinary destination. It features the city's best patio dining, a massive weekly farmers market (the Mercato), and the Piazza della Famiglia.

### Hillcrest
The heart of San Diego's LGBTQ+ community. It is known for its tolerant vibe, pedestrian-friendly streets, the massive "Hillcrest" neon sign, and diverse range of ethnic restaurants.

### Barrio Logan
A historic Mexican-American neighborhood located under the Coronado Bridge. It is home to **Chicano Park**, famous for its collection of colorful murals painted on the bridge pylons, which is a National Historic Landmark.

## Coastal Communities

### La Jolla
An upscale "village" offering luxury boutiques, fine dining, and rugged coastline. It is home to the University of California, San Diego (UCSD) and steep hillsides overlooking the ocean.

### Ocean Beach
A laid-back beach town that has resisted gentrification, maintaining a classic 1970s vibe. "OB" is known for its antique stores, the Ocean Beach Pier, and a strong sense of local community.

### Point Loma
A peninsula separating San Diego Bay from the Pacific Ocean. It offers panoramic views of the city center and historic sites like the Old Point Loma Lighthouse.
