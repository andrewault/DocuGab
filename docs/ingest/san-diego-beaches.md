# San Diego Beaches

San Diego is famous for its 70 miles of pristine coastline, offering a variety of beach experiences from bustling boardwalks to secluded coves.

## Iconic Beaches

### Coronado Beach
Located on Coronado Island, this beach is consistently ranked among the best in the United States. It connects to the historic Hotel del Coronado and is known for its wide, flat sands that sparkle due to the mica mineral content.

### La Jolla Shores
A mile-long crescent of sand favored by families, kayakers, and scuba divers. The gentle waves make it an ideal spot for beginner surfers. It serves as a launch point for exploring the nearby La Jolla Ecological Reserve and sea caves.

### Mission Beach
A classic Southern California beach featuring a lively boardwalk, volleyball courts, and Belmont Park, a historic amusement park home to the "Giant Dipper" roller coaster.

### Pacific Beach
Known locally as "PB," this beach attracts a younger crowd with its vibrant nightlife, pier, and surf culture. The boardwalk is always busy with cyclists, skaters, and runners.

## Secluded & Scenic Spots

### Torrey Pines State Beach
Located beneath rugged sandstone cliffs, this beach offers a more natural and rugged setting. It is popular for hiking in the adjacent reserve and for paragliding off the gliderport above.

### Sunset Cliffs
While not a traditional swimming beach due to its rocky coastline, this area in Point Loma is famous for its dramatic ocean views, tide pools, and, as the name suggests, spectacular sunsets.

## Dog-Friendly Beaches

### Ocean Beach (Dog Beach)
One of the first official leash-free dog beaches in the United States, located at the northern end of Ocean Beach. It's a popular spot for locals and their pets to socialize.

### Del Mar Dog Beach
Attractions include the North Beach area of Del Mar, where dogs are allowed off-leash seasonally (typically Labor Day through June 15th).
