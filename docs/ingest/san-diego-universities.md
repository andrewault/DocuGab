# Universities in San Diego

San Diego is a major hub for higher education and research, hosting several major universities and institutes.

## Major Institutions

### University of California, San Diego (UCSD)
Located in La Jolla, UCSD is one of the world's top public research universities.
*   **Known for:** Biology, engineering, oceanography, and medicine.
*   **Campus:** Famous for the Geisel Library (named after Dr. Seuss) and the Stuart Collection of public art.

### San Diego State University (SDSU)
The oldest higher education institution in San Diego, located in the College Area.
*   **Known for:** Business, psychology, and a strong alumni network in the region.
*   **Mascot:** The Aztecs.

### University of San Diego (USD)
A private Roman Catholic university located on a mesa overlooking Mission Bay.
*   **Known for:** Its stunning Spanish Renaissance architecture (often ranked among the most beautiful campuses) and its law school.

## Specialized Research Institutes

### Scripps Institution of Oceanography
Part of UCSD, this is one of the oldest and largest centers for ocean and earth science research in the world. Its pier in La Jolla is a local landmark.

### Salk Institute for Biological Studies
An independent, non-profit scientific research institute founded by Jonas Salk (developer of the polio vaccine). The campus, designed by Louis Kahn, is a masterpiece of modern architecture.
