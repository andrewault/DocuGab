# San Diego Attractions

San Diego offers a diverse range of attractions, from world-class zoos and parks to historic districts and beautiful beaches.

## Major Parks & Zoos

### Balboa Park
Often called the "Smithsonian of the West," Balboa Park is a 1,200-acre cultural oasis. It is home to 17 museums, expansive gardens, and the world-famous San Diego Zoo. Key highlights include the Botanical Building, the Museum of Us, and the Old Globe Theatre.

### San Diego Zoo
Located within Balboa Park, this 100-acre zoo houses over 3,700 animals of more than 650 species and subspecies. It is renowned for its open-air, cageless exhibits that recreate natural animal habitats.

### San Diego Zoo Safari Park
Located north of the city in Escondido, this 1,800-acre park features free-range enclosures where herds of animals roam in semi-arid environments similar to their natural homes.

## Theme Parks

### SeaWorld San Diego
Located in Mission Bay Park, SeaWorld is an animal theme park and oceanarium known for its marine life shows, thrill rides, and animal encounters.

### LEGOLAND California
Located in Carlsbad, this theme park is dedicated to Lego bricks and features rides and shows geared towards families with children aged 2–12.

## Historic Districts

### Gaslamp Quarter
A lively downtown neighborhood designated as a historic district, known for its nightlife, Victorian architecture, and wide variety of restaurants, bars, and clubs.

### Old Town San Diego State Historic Park
Considered the "birthplace" of California, Old Town features many original adobe buildings from the 1820s to 1870s, now serving as museums, shops, and restaurants.

## Maritime Attractions

### USS Midway Museum
A maritime museum located in downtown San Diego at Navy Pier. The museum consists of the aircraft carrier USS Midway and houses an extensive collection of aircraft.

### Maritime Museum of San Diego
Features one of the largest collections of historic sea vessels in the United States, including the Star of India, the world's oldest active sailing ship.
